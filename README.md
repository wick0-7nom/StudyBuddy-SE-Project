# 📚 Study Buddy

> A Campus-Based Study Partner and Group Collaboration Platform

**Study Buddy** is an Android mobile application designed to solve a common university problem: students struggling to find suitable and consistent study partners. By focusing on a campus-based approach, it connects students within the same university, department, and courses to make collaborative learning efficient and organized.

## ✨ Features

* **University & Department-Based System:** Secure user registration tailored specifically for campus students.
* **Smart Study Group Creation:** Create and join subject- or course-based study groups.
* **Study Partner Matching:** Intelligent suggestions to find peers with similar academic goals.
* **Real-Time Group Chat:** Instant communication system for discussions and doubt-solving.
* **Notes & File Sharing:** Centralized platform to share study materials, lecture notes, and resources.
* **Scheduled Study Sessions:** Plan and manage group study sessions with reminders.
* **Activity & Participation Tracking:** Track engagement levels within groups.
* **Instant Notifications:** Stay updated with group announcements, messages, and session alerts.
